## Design: never use
These read as machine-made. Leave them out of every project (app, landing page, mockups, emails).
- Purple-blue gradients.
- Inter on everything. Pick a typeface with a reason.
- Gradient text.
- An "Introducing" badge.
- A glowing halo behind an element.
- Frosted-glass panels and icon tiles.
- Rows of identical feature cards.
- Cards inside cards.
- Cards with a coloured stripe on one side.
- A giant "10M+" style stat.
- "01, 02, 03" labels on sections or steps.
- A pulsing green dot.
- Beige with an italic serif.
- The word "supercharge" and em dashes in copy.
