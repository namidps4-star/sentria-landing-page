Pharmacy

What pharmacists ask before they start

What file do I need?

A stock list: product name, quantity on hand, minimum stock, sales over 30 days, expiry date. Column names can differ from ours, you map them once.

Does it connect to my software?

Sources: a CSV upload works today. ERP, API and IoT connections are planned. Until then, export a spreadsheet and upload it.

Who sees my figures?

Only your pharmacy. Each account's data is kept completely separate.

Do I have to change how I work?

No. Keep your current routine. Check the alert list each morning before the first patient walks in.
